Ragdoll

The Ragdoll is a gentle and affectionate cat with the unique ability to relax all its muscles when held by its owner. By nature, they are calm, laid-back animals that prefer a sedentary lifestyle and do not care for vigorous activity. Although intelligent and easy to train, they often display a lazy streak.

Maine Coon

The Maine Coon is distinguished by its large size and thick fur. These animals have a large, square-shaped muzzle, a broad chest, high-set ears, and large, oval eyes. Yet, despite their imposing appearance, they are a friendly breed. By nature, they are calm cats, yet they make excellent hunters. They show no aggression toward people. They also get along easily with other animals and can act as "nannies" for small children.

Siamese

Siamese cats are characterized by elegance and sociability. They feature a long body, a slender tail, large ears, a wedge-shaped face, and almond-shaped eyes. Their coat is always short and possesses an incredible sheen. Siamese cats love to stay active. They have a complex temperament.

Abyssinian

The Abyssinian cat is a great choice for owners who enjoy an active lifestyle. Its coat resembles rabbit fur and boasts a magnificent sheen, yet it does not require frequent brushing. The cat has a statuesque appearance, featuring a medium-sized body, a slightly arched neck, a wedge-shaped head with soft contours, large ears, and almond-shaped eyes.

British Shorthair

British Shorthair cats have a calm and independent nature. However, they can be described as cats that are ruled by their moods; if one takes offense at its owner, it might seek revenge. Therefore, if there are small children in the home, it is better to consider a different breed. Their difficult traits also tend to emerge with age: the older the pet gets, the harder it becomes to please.

Scottish Fold

Cats of this breed can be either shorthaired or longhaired. Their forward-folding ears give them a unique appearance; these folds can consist of either a single crease or three. Large, round eyes give the animal a perpetually surprised expression. They have a medium-sized body, a short neck, and a rounded muzzle.

Siberian

The Siberian cat is characterized by a powerful body, long fur, and a beautiful face. These pets make excellent hunters and companions; they form strong bonds with their owners and get along well with young family members and other pets.

Burmese

The Birman cat dislikes noisy environments but doesn't mind interacting with household members. They tolerate being cuddled and display calmness and composure in any situation.

Oriental

Large ears and a regal bearing are distinctive features of this breed. They require a great deal of attention from their owners and will not let them forget their presence, even for a moment. Consequently, loneliness is not an issue—even for those living alone. They are devoted pets that love to play and are naturally curious.
