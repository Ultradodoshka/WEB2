Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry

The international wizarding school is the only school of magic in Great Britain. Education begins at the age of 11 and lasts for seven years. Exams are taken at the end of each year, but those at the end of the fifth and seventh years are particularly important for the students' futures (the O.W.L. exams are taken at the end of the fifth year, and the N.E.W.T.s at the end of the seventh). Schooling is free, but students usually purchase their own books and school supplies. However, there is a special fund for purchasing textbooks and supplies for low-income students.
